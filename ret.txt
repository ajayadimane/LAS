## 5.1 Finding deduplication and consolidation

The current pipeline emits duplicate findings. In one real analysis, about 50 findings covered only 13 distinct issues. The same issue appeared up to seven times, sometimes with different severities.

Before building the snapshot:

1. **Deduplicate deterministically.** Use a dedup key of category + affected metric + source page references.
2. **Merge evidence** from all duplicates into one finding.
3. **Assign one severity by rule** (Section 7), never by taking the LLM's label from whichever duplicate came first.
4. **Investigate the root cause.** It is likely that the Verification or Correction agents append findings rather than replace them, or that retries re-append. Fix it at the source.
5. **Add a test** asserting that no two findings in a snapshot share a dedup key.
6. **Separate document defects from business risks.** A defect is an error, inconsistency or omission in the DPR. A business risk is a property of the project, such as single-asset dependency or seasonal idle time. Defects go to Page 2; business risks go to the risk section and Annexure E.

## Critical corrections

Do not convert a hypothetical benchmark into a verified fact.

For example:

A harvester DPR may show 1,200 acres annually.

If the AI estimates 300 acres from 10 acres/day × 30 operating days, this is an **assumption-based scenario**, not automatically the confirmed realistic operating capacity.

Clearly distinguish:

- Verified source fact.
- Recomputed mathematical value.
- External benchmark.
- Illustrative scenario.
- Unverified assumption.

Do not automatically replace 1,200 acres with 300 acres.

Similarly, do not insert artificial cash balances merely to reconcile an unexplained cash-flow difference.

Every proposed correction must be supported by either:
- Verified source evidence.
- Reproducible calculation.
- Confirmed borrower information.
- Documented external benchmark.

## Priority mapping

Severity and priority are assigned by deterministic rules, not by the LLM. Implement these as configurable rules with these defaults:

| Priority | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Must fix | Changes project cost, funding or Year-1 cash available for debt service by 5% or more; or makes the DPR internally impossible (for example, capacity that cannot be reached with the stated inputs); or a document needed for submission is missing (quotation, missing pages, required registration) |
| Should fix | Internal inconsistency or calculation error below the Must-fix impact threshold; non-standard presentation that changes a reported ratio |
| Good to fix | Completeness or good-practice gap with no effect on the reported financials |

A material cash-flow reconciliation discrepancy must not be assigned a low priority merely because it appears in an annexure.

Record the rule that fired for each finding so the priority is explainable.

## Correction interaction

If the current dashboard supports interactive fixes:

- Allow the user to review a proposed correction.
- Display the original and proposed values.
- Explain downstream financial effects.
- Require authorization before applying a factual change.
- Recalculate dependent projections.
- Preserve the source DPR.
- Record the reviewer and timestamp.

Do not treat an AI-suggested correction as accepted automatically.
